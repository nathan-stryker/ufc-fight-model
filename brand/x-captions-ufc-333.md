UFC 333: Volkanovski vs Evloev, fight-by-fight captions
In walkout order (opener to main event). Each is under 280 characters.

Lucindo vs Caliari
The model's most confident pick of the night: Lucindo 75%. Caliari absorbs 5.88 strikes a minute, more than double Lucindo's 2.80, and Lucindo is 5 years younger with 4 inches of reach. Only knock: 14 months off. Likeliest result: Lucindo by decision.

Steele vs Gugnon
Gugnon needed 81 seconds to choke out Janicic in his UFC debut, and 10 of his 11 pro wins are finishes. Steele lands 29% of his strikes and absorbs 6.65 a minute. Model: Gugnon 66%.

Oki vs Santos
Two pressure strikers who both eat over 5 strikes a minute. Oki has 6 inches of reach but has lost two straight. Santos brings the wrestling and is 4-1 in his last five. Model: Santos 62%, most likely on the cards.

Vagaev vs Malkoun
Malkoun has won three straight and averages 5.39 takedowns per 15 minutes. Vagaev is 1-0 in the UFC and lands under 2 strikes a minute. Model: Malkoun 65%, and more often than not this one goes to the judges.

Thicknesse vs Chopurov
Chopurov debuts at 11-0 with 5 knockouts and 5 submissions. Thicknesse has won two straight but stops only 33% of takedowns. The model leans on UFC experience: Thicknesse 55%, close to a coin flip.

Dawson vs Aliev
Dawson is 12-2 in the UFC and 6-0 against wrestlers. Aliev is 4-0, 7 years younger, and stops 80% of takedowns, which is a problem for a guy who wants to wrestle. Model: Dawson 54%, basically a coin flip.

Magomedov vs Rowston
Rowston is 3-0 in the UFC with 2 knockouts, landing 5.52 strikes a minute while absorbing under 2. Magomedov is ranked 12th and has the experience and the wrestling. The model takes the unranked guy anyway: Rowston 54%.

Krylov vs Yakhyaev
Yakhyaev is 3-0 in the UFC, all first-round finishes, and averages 12.68 takedowns per 15 minutes. Krylov is 9 years older and stops 53% of takedowns. Yakhyaev 63%, and the model gives this the best chance on the card of ending early (74%).

Murzakanov vs Reyes (featured prelim)
Real power on both sides: 11 UFC knockout wins between them. Reyes has 6 inches of height and reach and beat Johnny Walker last time out. Model: Reyes 62%, most likely by knockout.

Pico vs Keita
Pico lands 6.16 strikes a minute and averages 6.54 takedowns per 15. Keita is the harder target and the bigger knockout threat. Model: Pico 52%, a coin flip, and if it's Pico it's most likely a decision.

Volkov vs Kuniev
Heavyweights who barely wrestle, so expect a kickboxing match. Volkov is ranked 2nd, has 4 inches of reach and has won two straight. Model: Volkov 58%, most likely on the scorecards.

Kavanagh vs Temirov
The closest fight on the card: Kavanagh 51%. Temirov is 3-0 in the UFC with the better striking differential. Kavanagh has 4 inches of reach and more UFC experience. Pick your side.

Yan vs Dvalishvili (co-main)
Trilogy. Yan won the last one, but the model still takes Dvalishvili at 56%. Merab averages almost 6 takedowns per 15 minutes, and Yan is 1-3 against wrestlers in the UFC. Likeliest ending: Merab by decision.

Volkanovski vs Evloev (main event)
Evloev is 10-0 in the UFC and 6 years younger. Volkanovski has the better striking (+2.68 per minute to +1.11) and is 3-0 against wrestlers. The model takes the unbeaten challenger, barely: Evloev 53%. Likeliest ending: a decision.
