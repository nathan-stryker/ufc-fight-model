UFC Fight Night 291: Buckley vs Malott, fight-by-fight captions
In walkout order (first prelim to main event). Each is under 280 characters.

Chovancek vs Yoo
Chovancek debuts unbeaten at 9-0 with 4 knockouts. Yoo is 3-1 in the UFC and a real wrestler, averaging 3.23 takedowns per 15 minutes. The model trusts the guy who's already been in there: Yoo 55%, most likely by decision.

Croden vs Chandler
Croden has the best striking differential on the entire card: she lands 4.27 a minute and absorbs 2.13. Chandler stops only 25% of takedowns, and Croden averages 2 per 15. Model: Croden 59%, most likely on the scorecards.

Anheliger vs Koslow
Koslow hasn't fought in almost four years and lands under one strike a minute. Doesn't matter much to the model. He averages 5.28 takedowns per 15 minutes and Anheliger stops just 27%. Koslow 59%, and the likeliest way he gets it is a submission.

Cerminara vs Stiller
Cerminara hasn't fought in 31 months and lost her last two. But she's 11-6 in the UFC with 7 inches of height on Stiller, a debuting 6-1 grappler with 5 submission wins. Model sides with experience: Cerminara 57%, likely on the cards.

Mahjoub vs Faglier
Two unbeaten heavyweights debuting. Mahjoub is 5-0. Faglier is 6-0, 10 years younger, and four of his six wins are submissions, which you don't see much at heavyweight. Faglier 63%, the model's second most confident pick of the night.

Leblanc vs Galanti
Galanti debuts at 8-1 with 4 submissions, and he's 6 years younger than Leblanc, who's 0-1 in the UFC. The model says this one is tied for the most likely finish on the card: 63% it doesn't reach the judges. Galanti 57%.

Boser vs Diniz (featured prelim)
Heavyweights, and neither has landed a takedown in the UFC, so this stays standing. Boser has the busier hands. Diniz has 4 inches of reach, lands 59% of his strikes, and is back after a year off. Model takes Diniz at 57%.

Nallo vs Landwehr
This one could turn into a firefight. Nallo lands 7.68 strikes a minute and eats 9.12. Landwehr has lost three straight, most recently to Cub Swanson. Neither wrestles much, so expect it on the feet. Model takes Nallo at 59%.

Jourdain vs Cuamba
Coin flip alert. Jourdain debuts at 9-3 with 4 knockouts. Cuamba is 2-3 in the UFC and coming off a loss to Sopaj, but he has the experience and 2 inches of reach. Model barely leans Cuamba, 53%.

Nelson vs Perez
Veteran vs prospect. Nelson is 35 and 5-6 in the UFC. Perez Gonzalez is 27 and debuts at 15-2, with 6 knockouts and 6 submissions. Model takes Perez at 57%, and the single likeliest outcome is Perez by decision.

Barriault vs Daukaus
The model's most confident pick this week: Daukaus 74%. Barriault lands 5.65 a minute but absorbs 5.94 and has lost two straight. Daukaus spends 41% of his fights in control, and Barriault is 1-3 against submission grapplers. Likeliest result: Daukaus by decision.

Blanchfield vs Jasudavicius (co-main)
Blanchfield is 27 and 8-1 in the UFC. Jasudavicius is 37 and brings the wrestling, but Blanchfield stops 78% of takedowns and outlands her 5.24 to 3.35 a minute. Model: Blanchfield 61%, most likely by decision.

Buckley vs Malott (main event)
Closest fight on the card. Buckley has lost two straight, most recently to Sean Brady. Malott has won four straight, most recently over Gilbert Burns, and has the better striking differential. Model still leans Buckley, 52%. Likeliest ending either way: a knockout.
